Image briefs and AI prompts

Every lifestyle image the site still needs, with composition, light, aspect ratio and placement, plus a ready-to-use prompt. Technical images (product close-ups, SafeMark) must be real photography and have their own brief at the end.

Rules for every AI image

  • Label it. Caption every AI image on the site: “Illustrative image created with AI. Not a GardenMark installation.” Never use one in the gallery, the product explorer or SafeMark.
  • Artificial turf only. The lawn in each scene must read as GardenMark-style synthetic grass, never natural grass. Check every output for clover, weeds, bare soil or mowing stripes and reject it if you find any.
  • Match the real product. Use the GardenMark product photos below as image references, set to low-to-medium image weight so they guide color and texture only.
  • Human, not staged. Candid moments, nobody looking at the camera, believable American households. No implied safety claims: no toddlers face-down on turf, no dogs panting on hot turf in full sun.
  • Deliver large. At least 2400 px on the long edge. Export WebP at quality 70–75 into public/img/ai/.

Shared style block (append to every prompt)

StyleCandid documentary lifestyle photograph, 35mm lens look, natural late-afternoon light with soft long shadows, warm but accurate color, gentle film grain, muted saturation, Northeast United States residential architecture, nobody looking at the camera, no text, no logos. Turf: premium artificial grass, 1.75-inch pile, blades blended in three greens (deep olive, mid green, light spring green) with a tan-brown crimped thatch visible low between the blades, matte finish, slight natural variation in tone, crisp edges where it meets stone pavers, timber edging or planting beds.
